Our Journey

15 Years of Hosting Excellence

From a small startup to a global hosting powerhouse — here's our story.

2009

Founded in Noida

QuickCloud was founded by three engineers with a dream to make hosting simple and affordable. Started with 100 clients and a single data center.

2012

Expansion & Growth

Expanded to 5 data centers across India. Launched WordPress hosting plans. Crossed 1,000 active clients and 50 team members.

2015

Global CDN Launch

Launched our global CDN network with 20+ PoPs. Introduced VPS and dedicated server solutions. Won "Best Hosting Company" award for the first time.

2018

Cloud Infrastructure

Fully migrated to cloud-native infrastructure. Achieved ISO 27001 certification. Launched 24/7 expert support with sub-2-minute response times.

2021

NVMe Revolution

Upgraded all servers to NVMe SSD storage. Performance improved by 300%. Launched 50+ country CDN network and expanded to 10,000+ clients.

2024

AI-Powered Hosting

Introduced AI-powered auto-scaling and security. Launched managed WordPress & WooCommerce plans. 12,000+ websites powered globally.
