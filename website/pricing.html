FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is web hosting and why do I need it?
Web hosting is a service that allows organizations and individuals to post a website or web page onto the Internet. A web host provides the technologies and services needed for the website to be viewed in the Internet. You need it to make your website accessible to everyone online.
Will you help me migrate my existing website?
Yes! We offer free website migration on all our hosting plans. Once you sign up, simply open a support ticket with your previous host's details, and our expert migration team will transfer your website with zero downtime.
Do you offer a money-back guarantee?
Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all our shared and WordPress hosting plans. If you're not completely satisfied with our services within the first 30 days, we'll give you a full refund (excluding domain registration fees).
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Absolutely. As your website grows, you can easily upgrade to a higher tier plan or a VPS/Dedicated server directly from your dashboard. Our team can help ensure a seamless transition with no downtime.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. We believe in 100% transparent pricing. The price you see is the price you pay. The only additional costs would be if you choose to purchase premium add-ons like premium SSL certificates or additional dedicated IPs.
